About

Isabelle Zhan

4 projects, 1 in progress

Second-year CS at UBC. Analytical by training, visual by instinct. I like building the kind of software that has an actual point of view.

I'm a second-year computer science student at UBC, and most of what I build starts as an excuse to tinker with something I'm already into. Nebula turns studying into something closer to a game because that's what actually gets me to focus. VibeSplit happened because my playlists became needlessly overgrown. HNSW's recommendation engine and MediaLibrary both stem from the avid cinephile in me.

I grew up in Vancouver, and in my free time, I'm often learning new dances (often those KPop ones) or chasing what's new: new songs before anyone else has heard them, whatever just opened at the theater, especially sci-fi. I draw sometimes too, usually when I'm supposed to be doing something else.
